<objective>
Build the shared E2E battery scaffold every later tier plugs into: a bash-3.2-safe
`tools/run-e2e.sh` orchestrator, the hoisted Lua harness (check/run_capture/scratch_dir/WEZ),
a generalized self-skip gate, the pure platform-expectations module, the `make e2e` /
`make e2e-setup` targets, a `tests/e2e/README.md` tier-matrix skeleton, and the one-line
`run-tests.sh` discovery exclusion that keeps the new e2e files out of `make test`.

Purpose: Tier 1 (Plan 02/03) and later tiers (06.7-06.9) consume these files instead of
re-declaring the harness. Correct discovery/aggregation + the loud-skip contract are what let
the deterministic tiers gate CI in 06.8. The run-tests.sh exclusion keeps the unit (`make test`)
and battery (`make e2e`) boundary clean so Plan 04's `make test` acceptance is not undermined by
double-running the e2e files against the stale PATH `wez`.

Output: tests/e2e/lib/harness.lua, tests/e2e/lib/skip.lua, tests/e2e/platform.lua,
tools/run-e2e.sh, the run-tests.sh discovery exclusion, the two Makefile targets,
tests/e2e/README.md.

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
