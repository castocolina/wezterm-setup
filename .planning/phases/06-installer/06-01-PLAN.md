   `nightly` tag carries NO datestamp in its name; the datestamp lives in the binary's `--version`.

   the per-asset `.sha256` round-trips (generate one one host, verify with the logic
   `download_release()` will use) and works on BOTH a `sha256sum` host and a `shasum -a 256` host
   (macOS has no `sha256sum`, Pitfall 4). This ratifies the contract change Plan 02 makes to
   `tools/build.sh`.

guesses. A failed spike changes the plan, not the shipped code.
