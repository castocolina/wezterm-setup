    regression), overridable via the optional third arg or a `WEZ_E2E_VISUAL_THRESHOLD` env var
    (documented in the script's header comment). Exit 0 when the parsed RMSE is <= threshold
    (similar enough — PASS), exit 1 otherwise (FAIL). Print the parsed RMSE value, the threshold
