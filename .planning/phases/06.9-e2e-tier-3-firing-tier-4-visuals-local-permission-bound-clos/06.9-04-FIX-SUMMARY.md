## Bug #1 — `spectacle -a` captures the wrong window

**Fix:** `capture_active_window(session, path)` now captures compositor
fullscreen (`spectacle -b -f`, `grim <file>`, `gnome-screenshot -f`) then
crops with ImageMagick to `xdotool getwindowgeometry --shell <window_id>`
(`WxH+X+Y` +repage). Crop output is rejected unless identify reports exactly
`WIDTHxHEIGHT`. `platform.detect_screenshot_tool()` is unchanged.

## Bug #2 — external `wezterm cli` mux mutations do not repaint the GUI

**Fix:** `probe_render_sync()` (same pcall + teardown-all-paths shape as
`gui.probe`) spins `gui.spin("probe-render")`, captures before, runs
`wezterm cli split-pane --horizontal --pane-id <first>`, `mux.poll_until` for
2 panes, captures after, RMSE-diffs via `tools/e2e-visual-diff.sh`. RMSE ~0
despite 2 mux panes → probe false. ANDed into the existing
`skip.require_tool` predicate (tools check short-circuits first).
