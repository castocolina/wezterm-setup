follow_pane_color opt-in, so saved recipes and ad-hoc scenes get the same identity
orthogonality the standalone CLI got in Plan 02 — keeping the SCEN-04
launch-equals-new equivalence intact for the new keys. Also lands the PANE side of
the cwd-empty title fallback (D-12): a colorless/labelless pane self-labels by its
launch-dir basename.

from cli/lib/color.lua, and the LOCKED follow carrier literal WEZTERM_TAB_FOLLOW_PANE
(W3 — payload "1"/unset, the same literal Plans 02 and 03 reference). No parallel
implementations.
